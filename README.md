Adds the reminessence injector, a new tonic that allows the player an in-world means of saving their game. Intended as a medium difficulty between classic and roleplay mode, to preserve the danger and risk/reward of classic, while allowing the player a second chance.

Includes configs for death saves being affected by normality, allowing for multiple uses of one save, and for allowing tonics to dynamically generate in the world. With dynamic generation on, the tonics will be roughly as rare as Eater's Nectar.

Currently can be found on Tszappur, in the Six Day Stilt, as well as on Barathrum, in the Grit Gate, and Santalalotze, in the Moon Stair.
